# upgrade-calendar-quick-add (U1704)

A calendar view uses `quick_add`, renamed `quick_create` in Odoo 17.0.

Applies to Odoo 17.0 and later.

## What it does

Reports `quick_add` on `<calendar>` views.

## Why is this bad?

Odoo 17.0 renamed it `quick_create`: the view fails to validate.

## Fix safety

Safe: the attribute is renamed.
