# xml-deprecated-qweb-directive (XML012)

A template uses `t-esc-options`, `t-field-options` or `t-raw-options`.

## What it does

Reports the QWeb directives `t-esc-options`, `t-field-options` and
`t-raw-options`.

## Why is this bad?

They were replaced by `t-options`.
