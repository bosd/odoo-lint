# upgrade-manifest-init-xml (U2016)

The manifest lists files under `init_xml`, ignored since Odoo 20.0.

Applies to Odoo 20.0 and later.

## What it does

Reports the manifest key `init_xml`.

## Why is this bad?

Odoo 20.0 only loads `data` and `demo`: the files under `init_xml` are
silently never loaded. Move them to `data`.
