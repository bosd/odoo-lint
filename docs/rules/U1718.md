# upgrade-ir-default-get (U1718)

`ir.default.get()`, renamed `_get()` in Odoo 17.0.

Applies to Odoo 17.0 and later.

## What it does

Reports `self.env["ir.default"].get(...)`.

## Why is this bad?

Odoo 17.0 removed the public `get` (deprecated in 16.0): the call raises
`AttributeError`.

## Fix safety

Safe: `get` becomes `_get`, with the same arguments.
