# upgrade-t-call-options (U2005)

`t-call-options`, removed in Odoo 20.0.

Applies to Odoo 20.0 and later.

## What it does

Reports the `t-call-options` alias.

## Why is this bad?

Odoo 20.0 removed it: the options are ignored. Use `t-options`.

## Fix safety
