# deprecated-self-cr (W8165)

`self._cr` is used instead of `self.env.cr`.

Applies to Odoo 19.0 and later.

## What it does

Reports `self._cr` in model classes.

## Why is this bad?

Odoo 19.0 deprecated the `_cr` shortcut; `self.env.cr` is the supported way
to reach the cursor.
